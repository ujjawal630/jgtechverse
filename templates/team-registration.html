04 / Guidelines & Rules

Standards, Integrity & Participation Rules

Key participation criteria to ensure fair competition, verified academic identity, and open-source project evaluation.

Team Size & Composition

Teams can consist of 1 to 5 members from recognized colleges and universities. Inter-disciplinary and cross-year teams are actively encouraged.

Mandatory GitHub Repositories

Every project submission must provide a valid open-source GitHub repository URL. Empty links or non-GitHub hosts are rejected by automated validation.

Multiple Project Capacity

A single team may register projects across multiple selected arenas. Each project has its own problem statement, solution, repository, and optional evidence images.

Privacy & Data Security

Student email addresses and phone numbers remain strictly confidential. Public verification pages only display team names, arena tracks, and approved project summaries.

06 / Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding team registration, multi-track participation, repository requirements, and ID card credentials.

Can our team participate in more than one arena track?
Yes. Unlike rigid single-track hackathons, TechVerse 2026 allows teams to select multiple arenas in Section 04 and submit independent project proposals for each selected arena in Section 05. Each project is evaluated separately.
Why is a public GitHub repository URL mandatory?
TechVerse 2026 values genuine engineering and open-source rigor. Domain judges and automated review tools evaluate repository structure, commit history, and code implementation to ensure originality and technical depth.
What is the permitted team size and composition?
Teams may have between 1 and 5 members (including the team leader). The registration form strictly enforces a maximum of 5 members. Inter-departmental and cross-year student collaborations are welcome.
How do we check our team's verification status?
After submitting, keep note of your unique Registration ID (e.g. TV26-00001). Visit the Verification Portal at any time to check whether your submission is Pending, Approved, or flagged for review.
How is our official Team ID Card generated?
Your official credential is generated automatically once submitted. You can access, print, or save your digital ID card with its scannable QR verification code at the ID Card Portal.
